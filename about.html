About PhysLab

PhysLab is a free website where students can learn physics by experimenting. Instead of only reading formulas, you can change the numbers in a live simulation and see immediately what happens.

Our goal

Physics is often taught as a list of equations to memorise. We believe it makes far more sense once you have seen a projectile arc through the air, felt how a pendulum slows down on the Moon or watched two waves cancel each other. Every simulation on this site is paired with a short lesson, the key formulas, suggested experiments and check-yourself questions with worked answers.

Who it is for

  • School students (roughly Class 6 to 12) who want to understand the ideas behind their textbook chapters.
  • College and engineering students who want a quick visual refresher.
  • Teachers who want a free classroom demonstration tool. You are welcome to link to any simulation page.

How the simulations are made

All simulations on this site are written from scratch using standard physics equations and run entirely in your browser. They use numerical methods such as Runge-Kutta integration for the pendulum and velocity Verlet for orbits. They are teaching tools: they simplify the real world (for example by ignoring friction or air unless a control is provided), and each lesson page explains the assumptions.
